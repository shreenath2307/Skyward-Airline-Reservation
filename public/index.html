About the project

Skyward is our DBMS course project: a relational airline seat-reservation system. Eight normalised tables hold every passenger, flight, seat and booking, while stored procedures, triggers and views keep each reservation consistent from search to boarding pass.

Live seat maps

Every cabin is drawn straight from the Seat table. Taken, free and premium seats update the moment a booking or cancellation lands.

Atomic bookings

sp_BookSeat wraps the seat lock, booking row and fare calculation in one transaction, so a seat can never be sold twice.

Automatic triggers

Five triggers keep seat status, available-seat counts and refunds in step with every insert, update and cancellation.

Insight views

Views for occupancy, passenger spending and top-rated flights power the dashboard charts with plain SQL.

Reservations that
keep their promise

Behind every boarding pass is the AirlineReservationSystem schema: passengers, flights, seats and bookings linked by foreign keys, with payments, cancellations, reviews and baggage hanging off each booking.

Fares, taxes, coupons and refunds are calculated in the database itself, so the numbers on screen are always the numbers on record.

Every non-key column depends on the key, the whole key and nothing but the key. Seat prices live with seats, fares with bookings, so an update is made once and only once.

A better way to book

Flight, seat map, passenger, booking with payment and baggage, then review. Each step shows where you are and what is left, and it ends with a printable boarding pass.

View each cabin by class, see which seats are free, edit individual seats or add whole rows in bulk. Booked seats are locked so they can't be picked twice.

Fare is ticket price × seat multiplier × passengers, plus 12% tax. Coupons WELCOME5, FLY10, SAVE15 and FESTIVE20 apply instantly, with 5% GST and a convenience fee by payment method.

Excess baggage is charged at ₹500 per kg beyond the class allowance, with generated bag-tag numbers. Cancellations run through sp_CancelBooking with a 10% charge and a tracked refund.
